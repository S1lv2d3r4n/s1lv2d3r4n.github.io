# Luces Siegas Easter Eggs

Easter Eggs are hidden features and codes found inside of  ~~El Salvador World Domination Simulator~~ **Luces Siegas**, which
  can be accessed with various methods such as the many Cheat Codes and Settings

## Cheat Codes
Cheat Codes (Called passwords in game) are accessed in game by pressing the **Insert** key on your keyboard
Here is an non-exhaustive list of cheat codes found inside the current version of Luces Siegas:
- **1969** - Turns the [Player](player.md) into **El Salvadorball** and declares War on ~~Pupusa stealers~~ **Hondurasball**
- **freemaduro** and **libremaduro** - Spawns a **Venezuelaball** at the center of the player's current chunk with all Stats set to the maximum `17`
- **jumpscarehn** and **jumpscare6** - Jumpscares the Player with a map of the 1969 Football War between El Salvador and Honduras
- **vendedoradeamor** - Changes the player's countryball's gender to the opposite gender
- **muertealamigra** - Sets the player's endurance `e` and speed `v` Stats to the maximum `17`
- **gib** - When followed by a number, it gives the player the item id for example `gib31` gives the player an Iron Axe id: `31`
